### Project Apple competitive advantage analysis

### What I learned
- An annual report is not a collection of facts. It is a source of evidence. The role of an analyst is to develop a hypothesis, identify what evidence would support or contradict it, and connect qualitative disclosures with financial outcomes before reaching a conclusion.
- Competitive advantage is more often the underlying factor, that causes a ripple effect in the revenue earning mechanism- like, brand-> primium positioning-> pricing power-> higher gross margin-> high NPM-> more cashflow.

## 6th August 2026 

### Project Apple Revenue Drivers 

### What I learned 
- Increase in installed base and upgraders are 2 different sets of customer bases. Installed base increase implies new customers aquired. Increase in upgraders implies, existing customers hvae purchased new upgraded product and the % of them has increased in comparision.
- Revenue growth and profitability have seperate drivers. 
- Driver – the factor influencing performance (e.g. installed base, customer upgrades).
Evidence – proof that the driver is currently active (e.g. management stating that upgraders grew by double digits).
Mechanism – the causal relationship between the driver and financial performance.
- Management often mentions evidences of growth not the underlying driver
  
### Questions I still have
- How to I measure these factors in numberic terms for valuation.

### Mistakes I made 
- Assuming every new product launches or technology innovated is the driver.
- Tariff costs affect the revenue of the product as well as the profitability.

### Skill developed
- Finding evidence and analysing a conference call. 
- Reading Form 10-Q.
